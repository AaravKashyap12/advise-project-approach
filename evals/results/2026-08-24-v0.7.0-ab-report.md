## Result

The treatment improved evidence honesty, explicit acceptance behavior, focused and broader checks, tradeoffs, alternatives, and failure conditions. It did not claim that proposed checks had run.

The treatment also expanded a narrow three-step request into a long full report, duplicated its action list, and prescribed a service layer without evidence that the additional boundary was needed. Its first numbered step emphasized domain modeling before the user-visible path, despite framing the overall plan as a vertical slice.

Two fresh graders saw the same criterion-level strengths and concision weakness, but each selected the candidate presented second. The comparative winner is therefore **inconclusive due to order sensitivity**, not a defensible A/B win.

## Follow-Up

v0.7.1 tightens the skill so narrow requests retain the requested shape and count, the first numbered step itself is end to end, escalation signals are observable, and structural patterns require project evidence.
