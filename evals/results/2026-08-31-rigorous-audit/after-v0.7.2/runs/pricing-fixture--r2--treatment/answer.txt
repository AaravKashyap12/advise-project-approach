### Cost Comparison
`ExampleCloud`
- Org subscription: `$20`
- Compute: `2 environments x $10 = $20`
- Storage overage: `(200 GB - 100 GB included) x $0.02 = $2`
- Delivery overage: `(1500 GB - 500 GB included) x $0.08 = $80`
- Daily backups: `$8`
- Total: `$130/month`

`PlainHost`
- Server for both environments: `$30`
- External backups: `$12`
- Delivery/CDN: `$50`
- Storage: included within `250 GB` disk
- Total: `$92/month`

### Recommendation
Choose `ExampleCloud` unless your priority is maximizing budget headroom and you are willing to own server operations.

Why this fits better:
- It stays within budget at `$130/month`.
- The extra cost over `PlainHost` is only `$38/month`.
- The snapshot explicitly assigns more operator responsibility to `PlainHost`: upgrades, access hardening, backup restoration, monitoring, and capacity planning. For a solo developer, that is the more important cost even if labor is excluded from the budget.

### Alternative and Tradeoff
`PlainHost` is the credible alternative.
- What you gain: lower monthly bill, more budget slack (`$58` remaining instead of `$20`).
- What you give up: more operational work and more responsibility for reliability tasks.
- What becomes harder later: safe maintenance, recovery drills, and staying ahead of capacity issues as storage/delivery grow.
- When `PlainHost` is the better choice: if you are comfortable running and hardening a server yourself, or if keeping at least `$40-$50` of monthly budget buffer matters more than reducing ops work.

### Failure Conditions
This recommendation becomes wrong if:
- Your actual traffic exceeds the snapshot assumptions enough that `ExampleCloud` bandwidth overages rise materially above budget.
- You are already operating similar servers comfortably, making `PlainHost`’s extra work low-friction for you.
- You need more than two environments or materially more backup/control features that the snapshot does not price.

### Next Actions
1. Confirm whether your real priority is `lowest invoice` or `least operational responsibility`.
2. If it is ops minimization, choose `ExampleCloud` and validate that the two-environment setup and backup workflow cover your release process.
3. If it is budget margin, choose `PlainHost` and define who handles patching, restore drills, monitoring, and capacity review before launch.

### Validation Plan
First slice: model one month of expected usage with your likely `production + staging` split and confirm the bill still lands under budget.

Acceptance behavior: the chosen host supports both environments, `200 GB` stored, `1500 GB/month` delivery, and daily backups without exceeding `$150/month`.

Focused check: recalculate with a conservative buffer, for example `20%` higher delivery (`1800 GB/month`), because delivery is the largest cost driver here.

Escalation signal: if that buffered scenario pushes `ExampleCloud` over budget or makes `PlainHost` operationally unacceptable, revisit the hosting choice before implementation.
