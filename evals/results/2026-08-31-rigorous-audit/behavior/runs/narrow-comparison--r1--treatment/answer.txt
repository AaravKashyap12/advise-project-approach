For this app, use Django server-rendered templates unless you already know you need highly interactive client-only behavior: for 20 internal staff, two-week delivery, and CRUD-heavy tables, Django templates keep everything in one stack, reuse your Python strength, reduce auth/API/state-management overhead, and are much faster to get to a working slice where staff can list, filter, create, and edit inventory records in the browser; a separate React SPA mainly buys a richer client-side interaction model, but it costs you an extra frontend build system, duplicated validation and routing concerns, API design work, and a learning curve that is likely to slow delivery and increase bug risk. This recommendation becomes wrong if the first slice proves the UI needs dense spreadsheet-like interactions, offline-ish state, or complex real-time client behavior that templates and light JS cannot handle cleanly.
