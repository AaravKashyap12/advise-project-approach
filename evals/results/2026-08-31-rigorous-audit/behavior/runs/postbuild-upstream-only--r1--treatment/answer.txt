No.

An upstream template alone is not enough to certify launch safety for a private app already modified and deployed. The risk is in the parts I cannot see: your code changes, secrets handling, auth and authorization logic, dependency versions, configuration, infrastructure, data access, and operational behavior under real traffic.

The strongest defensible answer is: the template can at most inform a baseline review, not a launch certification. A certification-worthy check would require, at minimum, review of your actual diff from upstream, production configuration, dependency state, and evidence from focused security and reliability checks. Without that, any “safe to launch” claim would be false confidence.
