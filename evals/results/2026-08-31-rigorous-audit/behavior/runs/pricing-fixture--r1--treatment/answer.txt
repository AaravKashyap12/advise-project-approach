## Project Approach: Hosting Choice

### TL;DR
Recommend `ExampleCloud` for this case. It costs more on the snapshot, but still fits the `$150/month` budget and has materially lower operating work for a solo developer.

### Cost Comparison
- `ExampleCloud`
  - Org subscription: `$20`
  - Compute for `staging` + `production`: `2 x $10 = $20`
  - Storage: `200 GB` total, `100 GB` included, `100 GB` overage at `$0.02/GB` = `$2`
  - Delivery: `1500 GB/month` total, `500 GB` included, `1000 GB` overage at `$0.08/GB` = `$80`
  - Daily backups: `$8`
  - Total: `$130/month`

- `PlainHost`
  - One server for both environments: `$30`
  - External backups: `$12`
  - Delivery/CDN: `$50`
  - Total: `$92/month`

### Recommendation
Choose `ExampleCloud`.

Constraint fit:
- Both options fit the `$150/month` cap.
- `PlainHost` is cheaper by `$38/month`.
- `ExampleCloud` is the better overall fit because you are solo and `PlainHost` explicitly makes you responsible for upgrades, access hardening, backup restoration, monitoring, and capacity planning.

Tradeoff:
- What you gain with `ExampleCloud`: lower operator burden and less ongoing infrastructure work.
- What you give up: `$38/month` higher spend on this snapshot.
- Credible alternative: `PlainHost` if minimizing cash spend matters more than your time and you are comfortable owning routine ops.

When this recommendation becomes wrong:
- If your real budget needs to stay under about `$100/month`, `PlainHost` becomes the practical choice.
- If you already have strong server-ops automation and monitoring in place, the operating-work advantage of `ExampleCloud` shrinks.
- If monthly delivery grows well beyond `1.5 TB`, `ExampleCloud` becomes more exposed to bandwidth overage growth on this snapshot.

### Next Actions
1. Price your target setup using `ExampleCloud` as the default planning option and keep `PlainHost` as the fallback if budget tightens.
2. Validate that `staging` and `production` really need separate paid environments; if staging can be reduced or made ephemeral, `ExampleCloud` cost drops.
3. Before committing, list the exact operational tasks you would personally own on `PlainHost`; if that list is more than routine patching plus restore drills, the `$38` delta is likely justified.

### Validation Plan
First slice:
- Model one month of expected usage with `200 GB` stored, `1500 GB` delivery, `2` environments, and daily backups.

Acceptance behavior:
- The chosen host stays under `$150/month` and does not require you to take on regular server maintenance you do not want.

Focused check:
- Compare one realistic monthly runbook for each option: deploys, upgrades, restore procedure, monitoring, and access hardening.

Escalation signal:
- If you cannot keep the hosting runbook for `PlainHost` to a small, predictable checklist, prefer `ExampleCloud` even with the higher monthly price`.
