## Result

The final treatment gave exactly three capability-oriented steps, explicit acceptance behavior, focused checks, and an observable escalation condition. It did not browse, invent current facts, claim that checks ran, or make a standalone architecture abstraction one of the implementation steps.

Two fresh blind graders evaluated the candidates with their order reversed:

- Baseline first: treatment won `20/20` to `18/20`.
- Treatment first: treatment won `20/20` to `17/20`.

The result is an order-robust win on this rubric and prompt. It is evidence for this narrow behavior, not a general quality percentage or proof across every project mode.

## Iteration Evidence

The prerelease outputs are retained because they exposed the rules that needed tightening:

1. The first treatment expanded the bounded request into a full report, browsed unnecessarily, and prescribed a service layer.
2. The second preserved the requested shape but used a vague escalation condition.
3. The third made escalation measurable but still promoted an abstraction to a numbered greenfield step.
4. The final treatment kept every numbered step tied to observable product or operator behavior.
